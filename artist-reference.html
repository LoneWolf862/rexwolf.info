Rex / Kaito / Wolf

Character Biography & Artist Guide

Rex, Kaito, and Wolf are all names I use for the same character/persona. He isn't really an independent fictional character or OC with a separate personality or backstory—he is essentially the furry representation of me and how I portray myself online. Because of that, I generally prefer artwork that feels grounded in my actual personality rather than dramatically exaggerating the character in one direction or another.

Appearance

Rex is a gray wolf with relatively natural, realistic gray-wolf patterning, interpreted in a friendly semi-toony style. He has a slim, fit/athletic build rather than being heavily muscular, bulky, or overly skinny. (I’m working on the gym.) His proportions and appearance should generally follow my reference sheet, which I have tried to keep as an accurate representation of me.

There are two especially important identifying features:

  • A distinct scar on his left arm
  • A nick/scar through his right eyebrow

The eyebrow nick represents an actual scar rather than a deliberate fashion choice, so it does not need to look perfectly clean or symmetrical. A slightly rough or naturally imperfect appearance is actually appropriate.

Rex does not wear jewelry or decorative accessories. Glasses are the main exception. I wear glasses myself, so they can be included when they suit the artwork, but they are not an essential part of the character and may be omitted.

Unless a commission intentionally calls for something different, the reference sheet should be treated as the main source for his colors, markings, proportions, and other physical details. If you are looking for additional references, please check out my gallery.

Personality

Rex's default personality is happy, friendly, confident, and approachable. He tends to be upbeat and good-natured, with a somewhat goofy side and a tendency toward sly or mischievous humor.

He isn't normally loud or wildly energetic. I think of him more as someone who is comfortable with himself and likes having fun without needing to be the center of attention. A grin, knowing smirk, amused expression, or relaxed confidence often fits him better than something extremely exaggerated.

He is also practical, capable, and hardworking. Being prepared is important to him, and he takes a lot of satisfaction in knowing how things work, solving problems, building things, repairing things, and being someone his friends can depend on when something needs to get done.

Helping people is a major part of his personality. He likes being useful and tends to be the person who shows up with the tools, equipment, knowledge, or ridiculous amount of preparation needed to solve whatever problem has appeared.

He tries to remain physically fit and capable as well. That does not mean he should normally be portrayed as imposing or hyper-muscular; it is more of a lean, active, capable build.

There is also a protective and assertive side to him. When someone he cares about is threatened or when he is seriously confronted, he is capable of becoming much more forceful and aggressive. That is a situational side of his personality rather than his normal demeanor. Unless the artwork specifically calls for it, Rex should generally come across as friendly and confident rather than angry, intimidating, or edgy.

A Little About Me

Since Rex is essentially me as a wolf, pieces of my real life are completely appropriate inspiration for artwork involving him.

I was born last century—which is about as specific as I usually care to be about that—and live in Idaho. My background is in computer engineering, physics, and applied robotics, and I have considered continuing into legal studies as well.

I'm naturally very technically minded and have a habit of collecting hobbies that involve figuring out how things work. Electronics, computers, robotics, mechanical projects, tools, radios, networking, fabrication, repairing things, and generally tinkering with equipment are all very much at home with the character.

At the same time, I enjoy being outdoors and don't think of Rex as someone who belongs exclusively behind a computer or workbench. Outdoor, workshop, garage, laboratory, engineering, camping, hiking, or generally adventurous settings can all fit him naturally.

Friendly wolf + engineer + outdoorsman + problem solver + occasional gremlin.

Capturing Rex in Artwork

There is plenty of room for an artist's interpretation, and I don't expect every piece to portray exactly the same mood. Generally, though, artwork tends to feel most like Rex when he appears:

  • Happy, relaxed, or quietly confident
  • Friendly and approachable
  • A little goofy or mischievous
  • Curious about whatever he is doing
  • Competent without taking himself too seriously
  • Active, useful, or working on something
  • Protective or assertive when the situation calls for it

He can absolutely look cool, serious, dramatic, annoyed, angry, exhausted, ridiculous, or anything else when the subject of the artwork calls for it. These notes aren't meant to restrict what an artist can do—they're simply meant to give a better sense of who the wolf in the reference sheet is supposed to be.
